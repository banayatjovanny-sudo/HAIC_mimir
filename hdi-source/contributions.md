##  Development Workflow & Commit Standards

* **Equal Collaboration:** Both team members review and approve code updates across frontend, backend, and documentation components.
* **Branch Strategy:** Work is performed on descriptive feature branches (`feature/`, `fix/`) and merged into `main` after joint verification.
* **Commit Guidelines:** All commits maintain descriptive labels (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `test:`) to track development milestones cleanly.
